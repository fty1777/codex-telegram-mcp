Messages and files pass through Telegram and your configured Codex/model services.
You are responsible for protecting your bot token and deciding what to share and
which actions to authorize. Enter secret answers to Codex questions in the terminal.

After pairing, only the paired user's private messages are accepted. Other users
and group chats are ignored. Local credentials, pairing data, message receipts
and downloaded files are stored privately on your machine; see the
[local state reference](development.md#local-state). Local account access and
management tools remain trusted.
